# Graphical Theme Style Guide — 0.6.22

The six themes change more than their colors. Each one controls typography,
component geometry, backgrounds, navigation, tables, status indicators, and
decorative graphics.

| Theme | Typography | Controls | Cards & Panels | Graphics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technical Blueprint | Monospace, uppercase drafting labels | Squared and chamfered | Grid-backed panels with corner marks | Blueprint grid and interface coordinates |
| Futuristic Interface | Geometric modern sans | Luminous pills | Rounded glass with blur and floating depth | Orbital rings, glows, translucent layers |
| Cyberpunk Neon | Signal-style condensed and monospace | Clipped neon controls | Sharp digital panels with offset glow | Scan lines, cyan/magenta signals, system labels |
| Formal Executive | Serif headings with refined sans controls | Fine bordered rectangular controls | Paper-like surfaces with gold rules | Executive seals, subtle texture, navy/gold details |
| Professional Enterprise | Modern corporate sans | Practical rounded rectangles | Crisp cards with restrained depth | Clear hierarchy and subtle accent bars |
| Industrial Command | Condensed uppercase production type | Heavy pressed controls | Steel panels with rivet marks | Hazard stripes, steel textures, mechanical accents |

All themes preserve the same page content, actions, fields, tables, routes, and
business logic.

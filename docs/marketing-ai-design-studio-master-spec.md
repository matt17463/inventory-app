# AI Marketing Design Studio — reusable creative system and acceptance reference

## Product intent
The supplied **Sequim High School one-page school spirit-wear flyer (November 11, 2026)** is an art-direction and information-depth benchmark, **not a reusable Sequim-branded template**. Every new client should receive original, client-appropriate composition and copy, informed by its approved identity. Never reuse Sequim logos, wolves, school name, purple/gold palette, prices, or contact information for another client.

## School spirit-wear — acceptance benchmark
The reference page demonstrates:
1. Professional Skilled Crafting co-brand header and local partnership message.
2. Dominant school identity: real approved mascot artwork, expressive collegiate typography, layered scene inspired by school geography and mood, high-contrast brushstroke headline.
3. Decorated merchandise heroes (realistic tee, hoodie, quarter-zip, hat, bottle). Use approved or authorized product photos and **user-reviewed** mockups only, with actual approved logos.
4. Three succinct visual program value propositions (program funds, online ordering, local production/support).
5. Four clear ordering and fulfillment steps.
6. A concise fundraising example including wholesale, retail and margin, but show **no dollar figures unless the user supplied/verified them**. Examples must state assumptions and not guarantee profit.
7. The substantial **bottom creative services module**: logo refresh/modernization, alternate logo versions, fall/winter/spring seasonal art, sport-specific options, club/activity/event variations; a before/after image only where two approved images genuinely exist.
8. Clear CTA and **verified** company contact, with accurate page-fit, full-color print output and legible typography.

The test is whether the generated design exhibits similarly rich, layered, product-led visual storytelling **and comparable information**, not whether it duplicates Sequim's artwork or colors.

## Client-adaptive creative pipeline
1. **Evidence/brand intake**: client identity, approved logo(s), verified HEX palette, optional mascot, organization type/age group, product photos/mockups, verified terms/pricing, contact.
2. **Creative direction**: local Ollama returns *structured advisory* ideas—mood, hierarchy, three different compositions, content suggestions and asset recommendations. It may not invent a mascot, asset, supplier fact, contact, profit, quote or approved logo.
3. **Asset composition**: original artwork/approved imagery, layered client branding, 2D apparel mockups with human-reviewed placements, optional client-approved hero scenery. Missing assets receive visible draft flags, not fake images.
4. **Data-backed modules**: benefits, process, fundraising example (optional/verified), logo customization/seasonal sports and clubs module, CTA. Editable and per-client; business rules control pricing.
5. **Live proof**: all composed elements visible at Letter-page size; avoid clipping and cramped 6pt text; compare against benchmark visually.
6. **Exports/drafts**: design parameters and private assets saved to client; print version visually matches preview, approval gates verified contact, brand, products and terms; no paid AI API required.
7. **Visual acceptance**: inspect full page at normal display size and print scale; compare hierarchy, imagery, copy density, footer visibility and identity fidelity. Automated content checks alone are insufficient.

## Rollout sequence
**Stage A — school spirit wear**: reach benchmark-level visual quality with the reusable pipeline for *any* school, not just Sequim, Greywolf or Helen Haller. These three are regression samples, not target-only clients.

**Stage B — adaptive marketing builder**: after Stage A visual acceptance, reuse the infrastructure with different art direction, imagery, modules and copy:
- Business: professional brand/uniform mockups, employee appearance, business merchandise, verified service process.
- Nonprofit: mission and fundraising storytelling, community visuals, approved impact data.
- Sports clubs/teams: energetic crest-led apparel, sport/team merchandise, fanwear, camps, coach recognition.
- Weddings/events: refined typography and personalized glassware, keepsakes, signage, gift packages and proofing process.
- General: premium product-led marketing with brand-specific sections.

**Stage C — QA and controlled release**: automated regression/security tests, physical-sized screenshot and print verification, manual aesthetic acceptance, then *explicit controlled production deployment*. Keep ongoing development and validation off production until the owner declares the feature set ready.

## Explicit constraints
- No paid OpenAI/image API required. Ollama local planning, optional future local ComfyUI imagery; deterministic fallback.
- Do not manufacture factual promises, fundraising profits, logo derivatives or photo provenance.
- Keep draft/compositor independent of production-ready manufacturing placement certificates.
- The original PDF is a benchmark, not a source of rights to reuse its branded imagery elsewhere.

# Skilled Crafting — Marketing Document Studio workflow (v7 drafts)

## Where to start
1. Open **Marketing > Document Studio**. Select an existing CRM client and one of the 35 document types.
2. Read the document-purpose guidance. The **Design Studio** makes a graphical **one-page promotional flyer**. For authoritative quotes, detailed catalogs, technical guides, approvals, intake forms, or fully editable Word files, choose **Open full Word/PDF document editor** instead. The classic editor retains the underlying 35-template native Word and PDF exporters.
3. **Step 1 — Approved merchandise:** Choose approved client photography or decorated Mockup Studio/WooCommerce output. Up to four images can be included; each can have a verified product caption. The logo and brand colors come from approved client information.
4. **Step 2 — Campaign message:** Edit the headline, description, benefits, process, call to action and contact. These fields are never silently populated from fabricated AI facts.
5. **Step 3 — Artwork services and pricing:** Toggle the special-design section and customize its heading, descriptive text and examples. Defaults are based on the chosen category (school, team, business, nonprofit, wedding, event, general) **and document type** (brand guide, quote, catalog, product guide, store announcement, etc.). You can describe logo refreshes, sport-specific choices, seasonal variations, personalized gifts, employee collections, tournament editions, or other services you actually offer.
6. **Step 4 — Generate concepts:** Generate three credit-free layout options, then choose one. Expand advanced directions only if needed. A separate optional button requests paid scenic background generation; approved logos and product images remain separate.
7. **Step 5 — Review and export:** Confirm actual images, required data, DPI, brand, prices and content. Edit permitted section positions or lock completed areas. Save the private draft. Explicitly confirm final facts/rights before printing to US Letter PDF or downloading editable print HTML.

## School, team and nonprofit fundraising tables
- Enable **Include itemized illustrative fundraising figures**.
- Add up to **four** named products. For each, enter its approved **product title**, **decorated wholesale selling price**, **customer retail price** and **illustrative quantity**.
- Example (illustrative only; not actual Skilled Crafting prices):
  - Gildan 18500 hoodie: decorated wholesale $17.25, retail $28.50, quantity 75 → $843.75 gross difference.
  - Gildan 5000 tee: decorated wholesale $8.50, retail $17, quantity 100 → $850.00.
  - Two additional products can be added. The flyer shows the breakdown and combined illustrative gross proceeds.
- Enter approved decorated wholesale, **not private supplier cost**. Prices are not AI-generated. Gross proceeds **exclude** taxes, processing, shipping, refunds and other agreed costs. Verify actual program payout terms separately.
- Clear any invalid line before client-ready export. The table limits quantities to 1–10,000 and amounts to two decimals.
- Old single-product drafts reopen, but when converted to the new itemized editor a product title is needed before sharing the revised calculations.

## Business, wedding, event and general product price cards
- Optional **verified customer product prices** allows up to four items with title, retail price and quantity.
- The flyer displays a customer-facing retail table and illustrative merchandise total without supplier costs or fundraising proceeds.
- This is **not** a binding quote; the full Document Builder supplies formal itemized quote files, client acceptance and documented terms.

## Saving and print safety
- Client drafts include service headings, descriptions and examples, product rows, product photos and captions, layout controls, and selected category.
- Changing any price, image, service description or other verified content invalidates prior export confirmation.
- Effective 200-DPI logos/merchandise and 150-DPI background requirements, overflow/collision checks, customer-data authorization and approved-source restrictions still apply.
- All 35 classic document types retain native Word/PDF coverage. The one-page AI Design Studio's browser PDF/HTML outputs are separate from native Word document exports.

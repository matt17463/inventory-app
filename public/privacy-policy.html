1. Who we are and what this covers

Skilled Crafting (“Skilled Crafting,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides custom apparel, printing, promotional products, online team stores, and related order-management and production services. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information across skilledcrafting.com, inventory.skilledcrafting.com, and related forms, portals, storefronts, reviews, and integrations that we operate (together, the “Services”).

A third party’s website or payment service may have its own privacy policy, even when accessed through our Services.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact and organization information: names, email addresses, phone numbers, organization/team names, and communications.
  • Orders and fulfillment: products, sizes, colors, customization requests, quantities, shipping and billing details, transaction records, invoice references, payment status, and customer-service history.
  • Artwork and personalization: logos, images, proofs, uploaded files, comments, player/recipient names, jersey numbers, and other details submitted for a design or order.
  • Authorized-user information: login identifiers, account roles, activity logs, contractor time records, and operational information for personnel using our management application.
  • Technical information: browser/device data, IP addresses, timestamps, cookies, and security or diagnostic logs produced when you use the Services.

Payment card and banking details may be collected by the payment processor or financial service at checkout; we do not ask customers to send complete card numbers through artwork or order forms.

3. How we use information

We use information to prepare quotes; create artwork and proofs; process and fulfill orders; operate team stores and customer portals; communicate about orders and approvals; prepare invoices, account for sales and taxes, and reconcile transactions; manage inventory, production, suppliers, shipping, and customer service; administer employee/contractor access; detect misuse and protect the Services; and meet legal and financial recordkeeping requirements.

We may use submitted content with design or AI-assisted tools for a requested mockup, quote, or project. Information sent to such providers is subject to their applicable terms and data practices; please avoid uploading sensitive personal information not needed for your request.

4. QuickBooks and other integrations

When an authorized user connects an Intuit QuickBooks account, we may access and exchange information within the permissions that user grants, such as customer records, invoice information, invoice or payment status, product/service information, and accounting identifiers, to support invoicing and reconciliation. We do not need your Intuit password to connect using the Intuit authorization process. Intuit separately controls its authentication service and related privacy practices.

We also use or integrate with services as applicable, including WooCommerce/WordPress for storefronts, Supabase for application data and authentication, Netlify for application hosting, cloud file-storage providers for artwork, email providers, suppliers, shipping providers, and payment processors. We share only information relevant to the service being performed, subject to the providers’ own policies and contractual terms.

Revoking an integration stops new authorized access to that service, but it does not automatically delete invoices, transaction history, or other records we must retain for business or legal purposes. You can request help with a connection or deletion using the contact method below.

5. When information is shared

Depending on the transaction, we may share necessary information with service providers (hosting, storage, payment, accounting, email, security, and design support), suppliers and shipping carriers, people you authorize to review artwork or manage a team order, and a school, club, PTO, or other store organizer when required to administer that organization’s store or distribute its orders. We may also disclose information when legally required, to respond to fraud or security incidents, or as part of a lawful business transition.

We do not publish private artwork submissions or personally identifying order information as marketing examples without an appropriate basis or permission. Our Services are not intended to operate as a marketplace for selling customer profiles.

6. Cookies and communications

Cookies, local storage, and similar technologies may be used to maintain sign-in sessions, remember preferences, support carts/checkout, protect forms, and assess service performance. Third-party commerce or analytics features, where enabled, may set their own cookies. Browser settings can restrict certain cookies, although doing so may interrupt sign-in and checkout.

We send transactional messages about quotes, proofs, orders, invoices, fulfillment, and support. If optional promotional communications are offered, you may request that they stop through the message or by contacting us. Operational messages may still be necessary for an active order.

7. Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to reduce unauthorized access, loss, and misuse, including access restrictions where appropriate. No website or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for order fulfillment, accounting and tax records, resolving disputes, maintaining the Services, or complying with law. Different record types have different retention periods. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to backups, legal obligations, and operational limitations.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to ask about a connected account or marketing communications. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain records when required by law or necessary for a transaction or dispute. We will respond in accordance with applicable privacy law.

To make a request, use the contact form on our website and identify it as a “Privacy Request.” Please do not send account passwords or complete card numbers.

9. Children and school or team orders

Our Services are intended for adults and people acting with appropriate authorization. We do not knowingly seek direct account registrations from children under 13. For youth teams and school merchandise, adults or authorized organizers may submit limited player names, jersey numbers, and size information needed to make ordered products. If you believe a child has provided information directly without appropriate permission, contact us so we can review it.

10. Location, changes, and contact

Skilled Crafting operates in Washington State, United States. Service providers may process information in other locations, subject to applicable protections and law. This policy may be updated as the Services or data practices change; the effective date at the top will reflect the current version. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate.

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, or concerns, contact Skilled Crafting through skilledcrafting.com/about/.
